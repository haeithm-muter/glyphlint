# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

This project exists because software routinely treats some readers as an afterthought. It would be a
poor joke to build it in a community that did the same to people.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind to people who are wrong, including when they are wrong about your language.
- Taking a bug report about a writing system seriously even when it arrives in imperfect English.
  Someone describing a typographic defect in their second language is doing more work than the
  person reading it.
- Accepting that a rule you wrote may need to be narrowed. A false positive is a real cost to a real
  person, and reporting one is a contribution.
- Assuming a page's authors were doing their best with the tools they had. This project scans public
  websites; every finding is about a page somebody built under constraints we cannot see.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Mocking a language, a script, a writing convention, or the people who use it — including the
  common version of this, which is treating a non-Latin writing system as exotic or difficult
  rather than as one of the ordinary ways human beings write.
- Using this project's findings to shame a website or the people who built it.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a GitHub issue, or privately through the
contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile if the report should not be public. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be
respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences determined by
other members of the project's leadership.

Enforcement follows the escalation the Contributor Covenant describes: a private correction, then a
warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban, with the response matched to the severity and
the pattern of the behaviour.
